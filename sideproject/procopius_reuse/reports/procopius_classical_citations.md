## 3. Prior scholarship recorded in Haury's prolegomena/indices

Haury's front matter records the earlier studies of Procopius's imitation of the classical historians, recovered here from the OCR:

- Braun, H., *Procopius Caesariensis quatenus imitatus sit Thucydidem*, Diss. Erlangen 1885.
- Braun, H., *Die Nachahmung Herodots durch Prokop*, Progr. (cf. also *Die Nachahmung Thucydides' durch Prokop*).
- Duwe, A., *Quatenus Procopius Thucydidem imitatus sit*, Progr.

These are exactly the 'correspondences researchers already noted' that the README's original plan wanted to list.
